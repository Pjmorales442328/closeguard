Bills in: photos of supplier bills, no typing

Two different AI readers read each bill on their own. Fixed rules then check what they read: the lines add up, the dates make sense, the invoice number is new, the supplier is on the approved list. Two readers agreeing proves they read the same thing, not that the bill is right.
